## What you will learn

A customization is only useful where it is delivered. This part compares distribution channels: committing files to each repository, publishing agents from an organization or enterprise governance repository, personal setups such as dotfiles, and plugins distributed through marketplaces.

The first lesson helps you choose a channel by audience and surface. The second lesson looks at plugins specifically: the available, installed and enabled states, repository defaults, and where enterprise managed settings take over. Managed settings themselves are covered in a sibling course; here they appear only as a distribution control.
