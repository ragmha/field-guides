## What you will learn

Customizations outlive the conventions they describe. Old commands, renamed directories and abandoned agents keep steering Copilot until someone removes them. This part closes the lifecycle.

The first lesson builds a stale-customization review from documented signals, such as instruction references, CLI discovery lists, audit log entries and skill usage counts, and is honest about what those signals cannot show. The second lesson retires or migrates a customization, including the documented move from Visual Studio Code prompt files to skills.
