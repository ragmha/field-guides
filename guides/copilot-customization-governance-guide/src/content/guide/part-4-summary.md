## Key Takeaways

- Customizations reach people through repository files, governance repositories, personal setups and [plugins](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/customizations/plugins).
- Adding a marketplace makes plugins available; installing and enabling are separate steps.
- Repository defaults in `.github/copilot/settings.json` can be overridden locally; only enterprise managed settings can require or block plugins. See [Creating and sharing a plugin marketplace](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/copilot-on-github/customize-copilot/create-and-share-plugin-marketplaces).
- Guide advice: choose the narrowest channel that reaches the audience, and keep one canonical copy.
