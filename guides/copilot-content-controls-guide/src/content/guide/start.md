This independent course is for repository administrators, organization and enterprise owners, and the security and legal partners who help them decide which content GitHub Copilot may use. It is a deep dive: it assumes you know the governance overview in the [Copilot governance course](/field-guides/copilot-governance-guide/) and goes further into exclusion rules, surface coverage, testing, change evidence, public-code matching and complementary repository controls.

Each part's summary has a scored quiz. Answer every question correctly to unlock the next part; you can retry without penalty. Progress is stored in this browser, not in an account. JavaScript and browser storage are required for progression. The quiz is a learning aid, not a certification or tamper-proof assessment.

The examples do not change production settings. Confirm current documentation, your plan, permissions and your organization's approval process before acting. Advice is labeled as such; product behavior is linked to GitHub's sources. All teams, repositories and files in the examples are hypothetical.

See the [sources and status register](/sources/) for every primary source consulted and for features that are in public preview.
