## Key Takeaways

- CLI and app sessions sync by default unless `remoteExport` or `--sync-session` turns it off.
- Business and Enterprise users need the "Store local sessions in the Cloud" policy enabled; otherwise sessions stay local.
- Synced sessions belong to the user's personal account; enabling the policy gives administrators no access.
