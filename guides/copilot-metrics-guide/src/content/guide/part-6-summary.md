## Key Takeaways

- Usage signals show whether people use Copilot; outcome measures and developer feedback show whether intended changes are happening.
- Use metrics to choose questions for developers, and feedback to explain movements in metrics.
- Report survey and interview evidence with respondent counts and dates.
- Start an impact report from the decision it informs, and align scope, period, baseline and population.
- Separate observations from conclusions, describe findings as associations, and record what else changed.
- Do not convert lines of code or time saved into financial returns; take spending figures from billing data.
- End every report with the next review date.
