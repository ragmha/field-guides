## What you will learn

Copilot reports include per-user rows, which makes it easy to build leaderboards and individual scorecards by accident. This part shows how to design a small set of adoption KPIs with clear numerators, denominators and windows, and how to publish them at group level with minimum group sizes, restricted raw data and explicit purposes.

By the end of the part you should have a KPI sheet that a works council, privacy reviewer or engineering manager could read without concern.
