## What you will learn

A count is only meaningful when you know its window and its rule. This part explains how daily, weekly and monthly active users are defined, why the dashboard and the reports use different windows, and how adoption phases sort engaged users into cohorts.

By the end of the part you should be able to explain any active-user number in one sentence that states its rule, its window and its scope.
