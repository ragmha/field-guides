## What you will learn

Copilot usage metrics arrive after a processing delay, cover a limited history and depend on telemetry that some clients do not send. This part explains the processing window, report history and signed links, then the coverage gaps caused by client versions, telemetry settings, network controls and unsupported surfaces.

By the end of the part you should be able to state, for any report, how final and how complete it is.
