## Key Takeaways

- Define every KPI with a numerator, a denominator, a window, a scope and the decision it informs.
- Take seat denominators from the Copilot user management API, because usage metrics measure activity, not license assignments.
- GitHub describes a weekly-active-to-license ratio above 60 percent as healthy; treat it as context, not a target for individuals.
- Do not treat industry comparison values as targets; the comparison is opt-in and suppresses small cohorts.
- Guide advice: publish KPIs for groups of at least five people, restrict per-user files to a small named set of analysts, and never rank individuals.
- Do not use customization fields to verify that only approved customizations are in use.
