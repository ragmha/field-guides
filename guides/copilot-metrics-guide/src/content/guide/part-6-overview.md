## What you will learn

Usage metrics show whether people use Copilot, not why, how well or with what effect. This part shows how to collect qualitative evidence such as surveys, retrospectives and support trends, and how to combine it with metrics and engineering outcomes in an impact report that separates observations from conclusions.

By the end of the part you should be able to write an evidence-backed update that states its limits and the date of its next review.
