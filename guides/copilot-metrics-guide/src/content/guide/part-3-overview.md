## What you will learn

Enterprise, organization and team views of the same people rarely sum to the same number. This part explains how a user is attributed to every organization they belong to, why only enterprise reports deduplicate, and how to build team views from the daily user-teams report without double counting or hiding small teams' absence.

By the end of the part you should be able to explain any gap between a sum of parts and a whole.
