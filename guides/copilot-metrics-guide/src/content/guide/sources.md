## Release status and scope

- Third-party agent app activity in `totals_by_3rd_party_agent`: agent apps are in **public preview - use with caution** and subject to change.
- The industry comparison is opt-in, disabled by default and available only in the impact dashboard, not in exports or the API.
- The potential ROI figure is a dashboard-only directional estimate; this course does not reproduce its method.
- Repository insights and the earlier generation of Copilot metrics endpoints are out of scope. Credit budgeting, audit logs and session data are covered by other courses.
- Capability support varies by plan, client and surface. Absence of a preview tag here is not a guarantee of availability or approval.
