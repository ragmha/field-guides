This independent course is for enterprise administrators, endpoint management teams and platform engineers who own Copilot client configuration. It covers what enterprise-managed settings control, which clients honor each key, how to deliver settings from a `.github-private` repository, device management or a file, how sources combine, and how to verify, change and roll them back.

Each part's summary has a scored quiz. Answer every question correctly to unlock the next part; you can retry without penalty. Progress is stored in this browser, not in an account. JavaScript and browser storage are required for progression. The quiz is a learning aid, not a certification or tamper-proof assessment.

The examples do not change production settings. Practice steps assume an authorized sandbox enterprise, repository and test device. Confirm current documentation, your plan, permissions and your organization's approval process before acting. Advice is labeled as such; product behavior is linked to official sources.

Client support differs by key, client and version. Check the [sources and status register](/sources/) for review dates and release-status boundaries.
