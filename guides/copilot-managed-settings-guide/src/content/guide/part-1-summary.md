## Key Takeaways

- Group keys by purpose: permissions, features, models, plugins, MCP, sandbox, telemetry and refresh.
- Some keys are defaults users can change (`model`, `permissions.defaultMode`); others are locks.
- Check the **Supported clients** column for every key before you rely on it.
- Several keys have minimum client versions; older clients can be blocked from connecting.
- Browser use is **public preview**; treat its coverage as subject to change.
