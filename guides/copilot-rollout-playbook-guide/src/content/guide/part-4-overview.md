## What you will learn

GitHub's [adoption tutorial](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/tutorials/roll-out-at-scale/enable-developers/drive-adoption) describes three stages: granting licenses, setting up the environment and learning to use Copilot effectively. Governance depends on all three: developers who cannot get access or cannot connect through the proxy look for other tools.

- Plan an enablement timeline and a support path.
- Choose a self-serve license request flow with a review cadence.

Champion programs are covered in depth in [champions at scale](/field-guides/champions-at-scale/); this part only places champions on the timeline.
