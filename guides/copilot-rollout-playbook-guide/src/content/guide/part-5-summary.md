## Key Takeaways

- Decide only with stable adoption, cohort data, a full billing cycle and representative feedback; otherwise extend.
- Recommend expand, hold or stop; give a hold a fix, owner and date.
- Expand from the pilot organization to more organizations and then the enterprise, raising or adding budgets.
- Roll back by turning off the policy, telling participants, confirming no usage, reviewing the budget and keeping the data.
