## Release status and scope

- Enterprise custom roles, used for AI managers: **public preview - use with caution**. Keep enterprise owners able to make changes directly.
- Included usage amounts and overage rates in the cost examples come from the pilot tutorial; confirm current figures for your plan before budgeting.
- Other capability support varies by plan, client and surface. Absence of a preview tag here is not a guarantee of availability or approval.
- RACI assignments, exception registers, gate checklists and review cadences are guide advice, not GitHub product features.
- Champion programs are out of scope; see [champions at scale](/field-guides/champions-at-scale/).
