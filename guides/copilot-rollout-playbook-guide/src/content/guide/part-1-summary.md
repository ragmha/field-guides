## Key Takeaways

- Expect legal, compliance and cybersecurity signoff; send each team the GitHub resource that answers its question.
- Give every rollout decision exactly one accountable owner. A RACI is an agreement, not a permission.
- AI managers (custom roles, **public preview**) manage AI controls but not access management, billing or metrics settings unless granted more.
- Review who can change policies; drift starts with too many editors.
