This independent course is for rollout owners, enterprise and organization administrators, and the security, legal, finance and engineering leads who share a Copilot rollout. It goes deeper than the [Copilot governance overview course](/field-guides/copilot-governance-guide/): who decides what, how to design a contained pilot, which baseline to set before anything is enabled, how to enable developers, when to expand, and how to keep governance current once the rollout is complete. Each part pairs steps from GitHub's [roll out at scale tutorials](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/tutorials/roll-out-at-scale) with a hypothetical team and a practice exercise.

Each part's summary has a scored quiz. Answer every question correctly to unlock the next part; you can retry without penalty. Progress is stored in this browser, not in an account. JavaScript and browser storage are required for progression. The quiz is a learning aid, not a certification or tamper-proof assessment.

The examples do not change production settings. Confirm current documentation, your plan, permissions and your organization's approval process before acting. Organizational practice, such as RACI assignments and review cadences, is labeled as guide advice; product behavior is linked to GitHub's sources.

Related deep dives cover specific controls in more detail: the [control map](/field-guides/copilot-control-map-guide/), [policy conflicts](/field-guides/copilot-policy-conflicts-guide/), [usage metrics](/field-guides/copilot-metrics-guide/) and [audit and telemetry](/field-guides/copilot-audit-telemetry-guide/). Champion programs are covered separately in [champions at scale](/field-guides/champions-at-scale/).
