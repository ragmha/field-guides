## What you will learn

The [pilot tutorial](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/tutorials/roll-out-at-scale/govern-at-scale/pilot-a-feature-or-model) ends with a decision: expand, hold or stop. It also describes how to expand in stages and how to roll back cleanly. This part applies both to the payments pilot.

- Decide when the evidence is sufficient and what the criteria say.
- Define gates between expansion stages, including a clean rollback.

For the metrics behind the decision, see the [metrics deep dive](/field-guides/copilot-metrics-guide/).
