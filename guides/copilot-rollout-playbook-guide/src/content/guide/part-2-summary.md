## Key Takeaways

- Define success criteria first and plan for at least one full billing cycle, typically four to six weeks.
- Features and models are enabled per organization; confirm every participant is licensed through the pilot organization.
- Estimate cost as people × expected usage − included pool, then set a ceiling slightly above it.
- An organization budget is a hard stop only with "Stop usage when budget limit is reached"; user-level budgets always stop usage.
