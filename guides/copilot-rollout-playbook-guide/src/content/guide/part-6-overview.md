## What you will learn

A rollout ends, but governance does not. GitHub's [impact communication tutorial](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/tutorials/roll-out-at-scale/communicate-copilot-impact) shows how to report outcomes in a way leaders can act on, and its guidance on [new features and models](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/enterprise/learning-about-new-features-and-models) explains why the baseline needs regular review.

- Build an outcome report that separates observations from conclusions.
- Set a steady-state review cadence with clear inputs.

For audit log and telemetry detail, see the [audit and telemetry deep dive](/field-guides/copilot-audit-telemetry-guide/).
