## What you will learn

GitHub's [pilot tutorial](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/tutorials/roll-out-at-scale/govern-at-scale/pilot-a-feature-or-model) recommends defining success criteria first and running the pilot for at least one full billing cycle, typically four to six weeks. Because features and models are enabled at the organization level, the shape of the pilot is decided by which organization licenses which people.

- Select a pilot organization and confirm who is licensed through it.
- Estimate metered cost and set a budget that caps it.
