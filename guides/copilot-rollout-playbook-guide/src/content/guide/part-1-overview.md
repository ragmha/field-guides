## What you will learn

A rollout touches legal terms, compliance evidence, network configuration, policy settings, spending and developer support. GitHub's [approval resources](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/tutorials/roll-out-at-scale/govern-at-scale/resources-for-approval) note that you will likely need signoff from legal, compliance and cybersecurity teams before rollout. This part turns that list into a responsibility map, then shows how to delegate day-to-day AI administration without handing out enterprise ownership.

- Build a RACI that gives every rollout decision exactly one accountable owner.
- Use a custom AI manager role, which is in **public preview**, to delegate AI controls with a clear boundary.
