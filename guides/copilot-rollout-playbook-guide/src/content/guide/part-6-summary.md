## Key Takeaways

- Start an outcome report from the decision, then the comparison, the evidence and labeled conclusions.
- Usage signals are not outcomes; do not convert lines of code or time saved into financial returns.
- Watch the changelog, review policy access and sample the audit log within its 180-day window.
- Guide advice: run weekly, monthly and quarterly reviews with named owners and a leadership report.
