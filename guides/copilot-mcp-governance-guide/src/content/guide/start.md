This independent course is for enterprise and organization administrators, platform engineers and security reviewers who decide which Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers Copilot may use. It goes deeper than the MCP section of the [Copilot governance overview](/field-guides/copilot-governance-guide/): policy scope, allowlists and registries, local and remote servers, credentials, cloud agent configuration, and the lifecycle of a server from review to retirement. Each part pairs product behavior from GitHub documentation with a hypothetical team and a practice exercise.

Each part's summary has a scored quiz. Answer every question correctly to unlock the next part; you can retry without penalty. Progress is stored in this browser, not in an account. JavaScript and browser storage are required for progression. The quiz is a learning aid, not a certification or tamper-proof assessment.

The examples and practice steps do not change production settings. Use an authorized sandbox enterprise, organization or repository, and confirm current documentation, your plan, your permissions and your organization's approval process before acting. Advice is labeled "Guide advice" or "Recommended"; product behavior is linked to GitHub's sources.

Several MCP capabilities, including custom registries and Copilot connectors, are in public preview. See the [sources and status register](/sources/) for review dates and release-status boundaries.
