## Key Takeaways

- The MCP servers in Copilot policy is disabled by default and governs only Business and Enterprise seats from the configuring organization or enterprise.
- Servers can be defined in user, workspace, repository, personal and custom agent locations, each with a different owner.
- Guide advice: keep an inventory of configuration locations before you write allow or deny rules.
