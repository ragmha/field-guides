## Key Takeaways

- The Agent sessions view shows the three most recent sessions; View all lists the last 24 hours.
- Filter by agent, organization, repository, status, timeframe and user.
- Usage records streaming and the usage records endpoint are public preview and limited to eligible enterprise types.
- Each needs its own policy: Copilot Usage Records Streaming or Copilot Usage Records API.
- Usage record bodies can contain prompts and code; set access and retention first.
