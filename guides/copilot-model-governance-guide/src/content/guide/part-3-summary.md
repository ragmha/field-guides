## Key Takeaways

- Hosting and data commitments differ by provider family; cite the hosting reference by section.
- GitHub states it does not train on Copilot Business or Copilot Enterprise customer data.
- Some models fall outside zero data retention; exemption approval does not enable them.
- Open-weight, preview and locally hosted models each carry their own caveats.
- With BYOK, the selected provider's retention and privacy policies may apply.
