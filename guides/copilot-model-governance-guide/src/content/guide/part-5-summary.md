## Key Takeaways

- Model price depends on token types, long-context tiers and time-bound promotions.
- Auto, Fusion and FedRAMP enforcement each modify cost in documented ways.
- Hand budget design to the AI credits process instead of repeating it in model reviews.
- User BYOK goes directly to the provider; enterprise custom models are a public preview routed through GitHub.
- With BYOK, the provider's retention and privacy terms apply.
