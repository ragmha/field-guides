## What you will learn

Learn how [Auto model selection](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/models/auto-model-selection) and Copilot Fusion pick models on a user's behalf, which models they never pick, and how their billing works. Routing does not bypass model policy: it chooses only from models a user can already reach. Your availability baseline therefore governs routing too.
