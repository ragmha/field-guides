## Key Takeaways

- Use one intake record covering status, scope, data terms, routing effect, cost and decision.
- Configure policies before a pilot starts, and run it for at least one billing cycle.
- Base models are enabled automatically within 60 days; review them before that date.
- LTS models are supported for one year; retirements list suggested alternatives.
- Compare approved models with actual settings on a regular cadence.
