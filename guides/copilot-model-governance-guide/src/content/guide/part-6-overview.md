## What you will learn

Learn to turn the earlier parts into a process: an intake record that gathers release status, data terms, cost and scope; a contained pilot based on GitHub's [pilot tutorial](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/tutorials/roll-out-at-scale/govern-at-scale/pilot-a-feature-or-model); and a lifecycle review that catches new releases, base model changes and retirements. Broader rollout sequencing is covered in the rollout playbook course.
