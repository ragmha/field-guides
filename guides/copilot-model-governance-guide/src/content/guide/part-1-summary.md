## Key Takeaways

- Model availability depends on plan, surface, administrator policy and time.
- Utility models run in the background and cannot be disabled individually.
- Preview models do not follow the GA default; evaluation models can change without notice.
- Record categories and release stages, and link to the reference for current model names.
