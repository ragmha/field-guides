## What you will learn

Copilot governance is spread across several surfaces that are configured in different places, apply to different people and are owned by different teams. GitHub's [governance controls overview](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/enterprise/governance-controls) lists seat assignment, network settings, Copilot policies, model settings, enterprise managed settings, billing settings, rulesets and audit logs. This part arranges those surfaces into a layer stack and assigns an accountable owner to each one.

- Name each control layer, where it is defined and whom it affects.
- Separate the team that configures a layer from the team that relies on it.
- Start an owner map you will extend into a full inventory in Part 6.
