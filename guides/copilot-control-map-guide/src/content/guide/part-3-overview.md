## What you will learn

Policies decide whether a feature is available. Two further layers act closer to the developer. [Enterprise managed settings](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/administer-copilot/manage-for-enterprise/use-managed-settings/deploy-managed-settings) decide what clients and agents may do once they run, delivered from a server file, device management or a local file. [Network access management](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/administer-copilot/manage-for-enterprise/manage-access/manage-network-access) decides which Copilot plans can be reached from the corporate network. Both are often owned by teams outside GitHub administration.

- Compare the three managed settings sources by reach and precedence.
- Record what subscription-based network routing blocks and what it does not.
- Identify the endpoint and network owners who must join the map.
