This independent deep dive is for enterprise administrators, AI managers and the security, network, endpoint and finance partners who share responsibility for GitHub Copilot. It surveys every control layer: enterprise and organization policies, repository settings, client-side managed settings, network routing, content exclusion, billing and budgets, and audit and metrics evidence. Then it shows how to keep an inventory of those controls, map them to risks, find gaps and owners, and decide which layer should carry a new requirement. For the broader introductory course, see the [Copilot governance overview](https://ragmha.github.io/field-guides/copilot-governance-guide/). Where a layer has its own deep dive, the lessons point to it.

Each part's summary has a scored quiz. Answer every question correctly to unlock the next part; you can retry without penalty. Progress is stored in this browser, not in an account. JavaScript and browser storage are required for progression. The quiz is a learning aid, not a certification or tamper-proof assessment.

The examples are hypothetical and do not change production settings. Confirm current documentation, your plan, permissions and your organization's approval process before acting. Guide advice is labeled as such; product behavior is linked to GitHub's documentation.
