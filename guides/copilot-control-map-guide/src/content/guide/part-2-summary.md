## Key Takeaways

- Enterprise policies are enabled, disabled or delegated; organizations choose only when delegated.
- Record each policy's reach, conflict rule and supported surfaces, not just its value.
- Default availability policies are controls and belong in the inventory.
- Rulesets apply to Copilot; organization and repository rulesets add to an enterprise baseline.
- CODEOWNERS, runner restrictions, workflow approval and the firewall bound the cloud agent's work.
