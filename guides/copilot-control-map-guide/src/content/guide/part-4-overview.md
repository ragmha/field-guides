## What you will learn

Two layers decide what Copilot may consume. [Content exclusion](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/security-governance-and-network-settings/content-exclusion) keeps specified files out of suggestions, context and code review in supported clients. [Budgets](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/billing-and-usage/organizations-and-enterprises/budgets) cap AI credit spending per user, cost center, organization and enterprise. Both have owners at several levels and both have documented limits that belong in the map.

- Record content exclusion scope, client support and limitations.
- Record which budget type caps what, and which ones stop usage.
- Identify the security and finance owners for these layers.
