## Key Takeaways

- GitHub documents eight governance surfaces; each answers a different question and none overrides the others.
- Record where each layer is defined, whom it affects and what it cannot see.
- Server-managed settings reach licensed users; machine configuration reaches anyone on the machine.
- Separate the accountable owner, the configuring role and the evidence owner.
- AI managers (enterprise custom roles, public preview) can govern AI controls without access to billing or seat management unless granted.
