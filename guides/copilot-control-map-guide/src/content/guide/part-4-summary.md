## Key Takeaways

- Content exclusion is set by repository administrators, organization owners and enterprise owners, each with a different reach.
- It is not a security boundary, and client support differs; record the gaps.
- User-level budgets always stop; spending budgets stop only if the stop option is enabled.
- The enterprise budget does not cap license fees, and budget limits do not remove seats.
- A feature can be enabled by policy and still blocked by a budget; record both.
