## Key Takeaways

- Give every inventory row a layer, reach, owners, evidence, known gaps and a review date.
- Read the risk matrix for empty rows, crowded columns and cells without owners.
- Place a requirement by its type, the people it must reach, override resistance and evidence.
- Prefer the narrowest scope; reserve enterprise-wide restrictions for non-negotiables.
- Combine layers when no single layer reaches everyone or proves the outcome.
