## What you will learn

Automations remove the person who starts the work, but they should not remove the person who approves it. This part maps the review gates that apply to automation output: branch protection, rulesets, extra approvals and workflow approval. It then sets out the switches that stop a single run, one automation, a repository or every organization in an enterprise.
