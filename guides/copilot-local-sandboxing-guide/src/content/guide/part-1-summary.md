## Key Takeaways

- Without local sandboxing, commands run with your full user access to files, network and credentials.
- Worktrees and trusted directories organize work; they are not file-access or network boundaries.
- Review unfamiliar repositories in Restricted Mode, and avoid trusting your home directory.
