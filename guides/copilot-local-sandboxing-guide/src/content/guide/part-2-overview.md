## What you will learn

Permission modes decide whether a person, an AI safety review or nobody approves each tool request. Learn how manual, assisted and allow-all modes differ in Copilot CLI and VS Code. Then track how long an approval lasts once it is given: for one action, the session, resumed sessions or future sessions.
