## What you will learn

Local sandboxing uses operating system restrictions to limit what Copilot's commands can read, write and reach. Learn how the filesystem policy resolves read/write, read-only and denied paths, and how network rules turn outbound access into an allowlist. Each lesson ends by checking the effective policy, not just the settings you typed.
