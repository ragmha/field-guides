## What you will learn

Every conflict investigation starts one level up. An enterprise decision either fixes a policy for everyone or hands it to organizations, and an unconfigured policy may follow a default rather than an explicit choice. This part shows how to read the enterprise state of a policy, what delegation actually hands over, and where default availability fills gaps that nobody configured.
