## What you will learn

A visibility report is rarely a single setting. This part turns the earlier lessons into a triage order: confirm the account and client, read the user's own feature page, trace the policy and seat sources, apply the conflict rule, then check resources and surfaces. It also covers model availability, which has its own statuses, defaults and an enterprise teams mode.
