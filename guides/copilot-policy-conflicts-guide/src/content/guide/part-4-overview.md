## What you will learn

Most Copilot policies follow the licensed user, but a small number govern a setting for everyone working in an enterprise's or organization's resources, and some controls are set by repository administrators. Policies also apply only on the surfaces GitHub lists for them. This part shows how to recognize resource-scoped controls and how to check surface coverage before concluding that a policy failed.
