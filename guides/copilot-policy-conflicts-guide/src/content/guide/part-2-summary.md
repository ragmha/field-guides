## Key Takeaways

- Policies generally follow the organization or enterprise that grants the license, not membership.
- Billing attributes a multi-seat user to one organization; each seat-granting organization is still a policy input.
- Enterprise-assigned users are not covered by "Let organizations decide"; Policies for enterprise-assigned users decides their defaults.
- Users removed from an assigned enterprise team keep access until the end of the billing cycle.
