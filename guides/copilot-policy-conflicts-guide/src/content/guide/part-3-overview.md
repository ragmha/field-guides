## What you will learn

When a user receives Copilot from more than one organization, their delegated settings can disagree. GitHub resolves most of these conflicts with the least-restrictive rule, but a short list of sensitive policies uses the most-restrictive rule, and users licensed by several enterprises follow different rules again. This part shows how to choose the right rule for each policy and how to recognize the documented exceptions.
