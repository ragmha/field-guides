This independent course is for enterprise owners, organization owners, AI managers and support engineers who need to explain the effective Copilot policy for a specific person. It goes deeper than the policy-scope lesson in the [governance overview course](/field-guides/copilot-governance-guide/): delegation states, seat sources, enterprise-assigned users, least- and most-restrictive rules and their documented exceptions, policies that govern resources rather than users, troubleshooting, and the records that make each decision reviewable. All scenarios are hypothetical.

Each part's summary has a scored quiz. Answer every question correctly to unlock the next part; you can retry without penalty. Progress is stored in this browser, not in an account. JavaScript and browser storage are required for progression. The quiz is a learning aid, not a certification or tamper-proof assessment.

The examples do not change production settings. Confirm current documentation, your plan, permissions and your organization's approval process before acting. Advice is labeled as such; product behavior is linked to GitHub's sources. A prediction from these rules is not an observation; confirm the effective result on the actual surface before you call it verified.
