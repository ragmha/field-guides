## Key Takeaways

- Start at the enterprise. A fixed state, or a selected-organizations scope, cannot be overridden by an organization owner.
- Only a delegated policy makes the organization setting a deciding input.
- An unconfigured GA feature follows the default policy for new features and clients; preview features and documented exclusions do not.
- Record whether a state is explicit or inherited from a default.
